Reduce JPG File Size — Free Online Compressor

Need to reduce the size of your JPG images for email, web upload, or social media? Our free Reduce JPG tool lets you compress JPEG files instantly — right in your browser, with zero uploads to any server.

Two Compression Modes

By Quality: Drag the quality slider to balance file size vs. visual quality. Lower quality = smaller file. Great when you just need files "small enough".

By Target Size: Specify the exact file size you want (e.g., 100 KB, 200 KB, 1 MB). The tool automatically finds the best quality to hit your target without visible artifacts.
